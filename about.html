About ASD International

Pharmaceutical rigor, regional commitment.

A leading UAE-based medical company with ambitious plans across the GCC and the Middle East — built on quality, science and care.

Our story

From Abu Dhabi, for the region.

ASD International Medical Requisites LLC is a leading medical company based in the United Arab Emirates. Our headquarters in ICAD III, Abu Dhabi, gives us the manufacturing rigor and regulatory access to serve healthcare professionals across the UAE and the wider GCC.

Our unwavering commitment to quality is what sets us apart. We believe that patients in our region deserve nothing but the best service and care — supplements engineered with the rigor of pharmaceuticals and the bioavailability of natural wellness.

Every product in our portfolio is built on advanced delivery technology — chelation, sustained release, liposomal carriers and nanotechnology — designed to make every milligram count.

What we believe

Our mission, in three commitments.

Bridge science and wellness

To develop and deliver innovative, science-backed nutritional supplements that bridge the gap between pharmaceutical rigor and natural wellness.

Serve the region

To make pharmaceutical-grade supplements accessible across the GCC and the Middle East — through trusted partnerships with pharmacies, hospitals and distributors.

Hold the line on quality

Every batch is held to strict quality controls. Every formula is reviewed by medical experts. Every claim is backed by science.

Technologies we work with

The science inside every ASD product.

Liposomal delivery

Lipid-based carriers protect active ingredients through digestion, dramatically improving absorption and tolerability — used in ASD Iron, ASD Magnesium and the Semifer range.

Chelation

Minerals bound to amino acids (e.g. iron bisglycinate in ASD Chelazen) for superior bioavailability and gentler GI tolerance.

Sustained release

Patented technology that releases nutrients gradually over the course of the day — for steady energy, focus and wellness, as in ASD Sustained Vit.

Nanotechnology

Particle-size engineering that increases surface area and absorption efficiency, used across the ASD Iron and Chelazen formulations.

Patent formulas

Selected combinations — such as Zinco Q10 — are patent-protected formulas designed for synergistic action.

Pharmaceutical-grade ingredients

Sourced and verified at pharmaceutical standards, with batch-level traceability and rigorous quality control.
